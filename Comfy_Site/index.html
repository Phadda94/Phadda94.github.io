Divine Choice is a 2D action roguelite adventure where you step into a role of cosmic importance, wielding the power of both divine grace and infernal wrath. Your decisions, whether to vanquish your foes with deadly weaponry or to spare them, shape not just your arsenal and rewards, but the very world around you and the unfolding narrative. This dualistic approach creates a dynamic and responsive environment, turning each choice into a pivot between salvation and destruction, making every playthrough a unique exploration of morality and consequence.
